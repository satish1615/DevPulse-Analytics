03 / DATA & METHODOLOGY

Transparent by design.

Source: Stack Overflow Developer Survey 2025 official published aggregate data, adapted under the ODbL 1.0. This dashboard contains 795 normalized aggregate observations across 11 questions, rather than individual responses. We validate numeric counts, remove nonnumeric chart records, standardize labels, and preserve response denominators. Multi-select questions can sum beyond 100%. Question bases differ, and missing or unreported choices may mean displayed bars do not total 100%. Survey respondents are self-selected, so results are descriptive, not population estimates or causal claims.
